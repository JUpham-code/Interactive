Auguste Rodin

I admire Auguste Rodin’s ability to convey feelings (such as resilience in The Walking Man) through intriguing means and with great detail, whilst using such unforgiving mediums, such as the bronze used in this statue.



Bill Hicks

The ability to make people laugh themselves into greater understanding is a skill I've seen few able to accomplish, and none as well as Bill Hicks, the comedian and musician who weaved philosophy into jokes about the ridiculousness of life.



Beck

Beck brings a fascinating style to his creation of music videos and songs which many see as chaotic and strange, and rightfully so, but he never lets others judgement dissuade him and still somehow connects to unexplainable feelings within his audience .





Reflection


I decided to organize the page by first putting my name, followed by the title of the section, and then the names of the artists, then their image, and lastly the description.
I chose to hyperlink both the name and the image for each artist, which I like the feel of. Though, I first made the mistake of not making my hyperlinks open a new tab when clicked, and found out how very annoying that can be when trying to go back and forth on the website. Thankfully, they all worked, but the user experience was certainly lacking.
An interesting connection between all of the artists that I had not considered before was how though they all had different mediums, they all found ways to express seemingly indescribable emotions that deeply resonated with their audiences.
I like the setup of the information within the website, but am not a fan of how large the images are, and would like the different sections to be within their own background shapes, with their own colors to identify them.
