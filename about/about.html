Biography


I'm from San Jose, California in the United States.

I'm currently enrolled at San Francisco State University, double majoring in both Industrial Design with a concentration in Product Design and Development, as well as Visual Communication Design. I am also minoring in International Business and working towards a Certification in Multilingual and International Business Competencies.

My creative interests vary from that of musical instruments, predominantly guitar, to painting from time to time.

At the moment, I don't make much beoyond what is necessary for the classes I am taking. In the future, I hope to be able to design a myriad of different products, from cars, to phones, to space stations.



I am taking this class because it is a core requirement for the Visual Communication Design Major, and also because I'd like to be able to make my own websites to better advertise my capabilities in my own portfolio, in the future.

Reflection


During the creation of this page, I had to create a YouTube page, which I had never done before, so that was a neat experience. I also found it hard to find what I wanted to use as a video self-portrait and ended up choosing what I felt was a natural and relaxing video, because I feel that encapsulates an aspect of me that is hard to put into and express in words, but video allows to be seen easily.
I ended up choosing a video that was taken of me hanging out with some international friends and my roommate, where I'm playing guitar on the couch as we all hang out, exhausted and tired after a busy week. Sadly, I don't find myself playing guitar too often anymore, but it's something I wish I did more often, and hope to pick up again.
I placed the link at the bottom, because I always liked the placement of socials at the bottom of the page. It just feels natural. Though, if I could change some things, I'd definitely add more color, and a background image of the pacific northwest, because it's an area I love that gives off the same vibes I'm trying to incorporate in the site.
