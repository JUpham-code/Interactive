mid-century modern

02/14/2024

a visual guide to mid-century modern style

I tried to color the margins at first, before I found that there wasn’t really a way to do that (at least that I could find.) So, I then resorted to making large colored borders for the page which I’m pretty happy with how they came out, but there is still a tiny little strip of orange at the top of the page, which I am not quite sure how to get rid of. I’m not quite sure if I have all the ratios right, as for the amount of color in relation to the text or if the amount of orange on the sides is wide enough, because I couldn’t really get a good sense of it from the perspective of the image. I tried my best at adding in the fonts, and they seem to have worked in some parts for sure, but others, I can’t really tell to be honest. However, I did end up learning just how important fonts really are, as they changed the whole vibe of the article, and are definitely something I’m going to pay much more attention to when implementing in future projects, and the sketchbook website as a whole. I also noticed how simple additions of colored lines and specifically applied indentation can completely alter the feeling of the site as a whole, making it feel much more professional and put together. I would say that the only part that was difficult to reproduce, and I’m still unsure if I have correctly, was the orange borders directly as intended from the image.
